Uitval Slips is installed.

WHERE YOUR SLIPS ARE KEPT

   C:\ProgramData\UitvalSlips

   The slip database, every automatic backup, and your licence live there - NOT in
   the program folder. That separation is deliberate: installing a newer version
   replaces the program and never touches your records, and uninstalling leaves them
   behind rather than taking them with it.


FIRST TIME YOU OPEN IT

   1. A setup screen asks for your company name, printer, and paper size.
      Fill it in and click Get Started.

   2. The program will ask you to license this computer. It shows a code - send that
      code to the address on screen and paste back the licence you are sent. Until
      then you can open, search, print and export slips, but not create new ones.

   3. Set the printing up before you issue real slips. Printer Settings, then
      Calibration Page, then Print Test Page - measure where it lands and adjust.
      Slips can be voided but never deleted, so anything you make while
      experimenting stays on record for good.

   No printer? Choose "Microsoft Print to PDF" and everything works to a file.


UPDATING LATER

   Run the newer setup.exe over the top. Do not uninstall first - just install. Your
   slips, backups, licence and printer calibration all carry across untouched.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG

   The Main Page has a Restore Backup button. It lists every backup with its date and
   how many slips are in it, sets your current database safely aside rather than
   overwriting it, and puts the chosen one back.

   Backups are taken automatically when the program opens and as you work, so the
   most you can lose is a few minutes.
