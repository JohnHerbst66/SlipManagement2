Hi [name],

Here is the truck slip program to install.

INSTALLING

Run UitvalSlips-Setup and follow the steps. It puts the program in
C:\Program Files\Uitval Slips and makes a desktop shortcut.

Two things you will see on the way:

  - Windows may show a blue box saying "Windows protected your PC". That is
    only because the program did not come from the Microsoft Store. Click
    "More info", then "Run anyway".

  - It will ask you to accept the licence before it continues. Please do read
    it. The short version: it is free, it is licensed to one computer at one
    site, and your slips remain yours.

You will also be asked for an administrator password, because it installs for
everyone on the PC rather than just one user.

FIRST TIME YOU OPEN IT

1. A setup screen asks for your company name, printer and paper size.

2. It will then ask you to license this computer. A code is shown on screen -
   send me that code and I will send a licence straight back. Paste it in and
   click Activate. Until then you can open, search, print and export slips,
   but not create new ones.

3. Please set the printing up before you start issuing real slips. Go to
   Printer Settings, then Calibration Page, then Print Test Page, and measure
   where it actually lands. Slips can be cancelled but never deleted, so
   anything you create while experimenting stays on the record permanently.

   No printer to hand? Choose "Microsoft Print to PDF" and it all works to a
   file instead.

YOUR SLIPS ARE KEPT SEPARATELY

Everything you enter is stored in C:\ProgramData\UitvalSlips, not in the
program folder. That means when I send you an update, you just run the new
installer over the top - do not uninstall first - and your slips, your licence
and your printer settings all carry across untouched.

The program also backs itself up as you work. If anything ever goes wrong there
is a "Restore Backup" button on the main screen that lists every backup with
its date and how many slips are in it.

IF SOMETHING LOOKS WRONG

Tell me what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead. A photo of
the screen helps more than anything.
